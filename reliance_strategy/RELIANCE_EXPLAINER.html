ATR & 20-Min Move Detection
Reliance 15 Oct, 9:15–11:00 (asli candles)
Logic:
  • ATR(14) = pichli 14 candle ka average range. Reliance ke liye aksar ₹2–4.
  • Move = 4 candle (20 min) me kitna chala ÷ ATR
  • Move +1.5 se upar = tez upar bhaaga → BUY
  • Move −1.5 se neeche = tez gira → SELL
ATR se divide isliye, taaki shaant aur tez dono din pe same rule chale.
6
Strategy Logic (Momentum + Trailing Stop)

1. ATR

Reliance normally ek candle me kitna hilta hai — ye "ruler" hai.

2. 20-Min Move

Pichle 4 candle ka move, ATR ke hisaab se naapo.

3. Signal

Move > +1.5 → BUY, Move < −1.5 → SELL. 9:30–14:30, din me 1.

4. Entry (5-Min)

Signal candle band → agli candle ke OPEN pe entry, SL 2×ATR.
Complete Setup: ATR → 20-Min Move → Signal (±1.5) → Next Candle Entry → 2×ATR Trailing SL → Exit (SL / 15:15)
7
Trade Management
Entry ke baad bot khud trade sambhalta hai:
  • Stop loss: entry se 2×ATR door
  • Har candle close pe: SL = Close − 2×ATR (BUY) / Close + 2×ATR (SELL)
  • SL sirf favour me khiskta hai, kabhi peeche nahi
  • Koi fixed target nahi — bada move chalne do
  • 15:15 pe jo bacha, band • Din me sirf 1 trade
Example (15 Oct): Entry ₹1181.5 • ATR ₹3.03 • SL ₹1175.4 (risk ₹6.1)
Price upar ₹1218.8 tak gaya, SL khiskte-khiskte ₹1212.5 pe hit
Profit ₹31.0/share × 100 = ₹3,104 → charges ke baad ₹3,033 (+2.57%)
